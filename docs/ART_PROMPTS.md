# Dubai Nights — Prompt Pack 1 (Prototype: Karama, first 30 days)

These are the images for the first playable version: you land at DXB on a visit visa, live in a Karama bedspace, drive as a ride-app captain and have 30 days to get sponsored.

**How we build it (same as Gully to Glory)**
- The game is plain HTML/CSS/JS inside an Android WebView shell, the same setup as Gully to Glory. We reuse its proven parts: story engine, choices, stats, AdMob, notifications, Manglish/Hinglish languages, server chapters and the Play Store kit.
- The **2.5D look comes from the art, not the engine**: every scene is a stylised 3D "diorama" seen from a three-quarter top-down camera. Characters are cut-out portraits on top, as in Gully to Glory.

**How to use**
- Copy one block at a time. Each prompt already includes the style and the size. Pick **9:16** every time (only E2 and E3 are **1:1**).
- **Make A1 first and keep the one you like best.** It sets the look for everything else. When you make the other characters, attach A1 with **+** and add "match the art style of the reference image" so the whole cast looks like one game.
- **Expressions (B):** attach that character's A image so it's the same person.
- Send them back with the file name (for example "char_rasheed"). I'll cut out the green, resize them (backgrounds 768×1376, characters 1100 tall), compress them to WebP and put them in.

**The look (already written into every prompt)**
- Colours from Dubai's city brand: electric blue **#0090D7**, magenta **#BB2C88** and deep black, with warm white light from windows and street lamps. We use the colours only, never the logo.
- Stylised 3D, clean shapes, smooth matte shading. Not photoreal, not pixel art, not flat cartoon.
- **No text, no logos, no real brand names** anywhere in the images. Shop signs and screens stay blank or show shapes only. Real brands come later, only with signed deals.

**Where the images get cropped**
- **Backgrounds (C):** characters stand in front of the bottom 70% and the dialogue box covers the bottom 25%. Put the interesting part (skyline, lights, signs) in the **top half**.
- **Characters (A, B):** shown from the knees up. Avoid green clothes and green objects: they disappear when I cut out the background.
